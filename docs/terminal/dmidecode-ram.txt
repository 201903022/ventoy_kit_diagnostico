title: Cuánta RAM cabe y qué ranuras están libres (HP EliteDesk 800 G1)
$ sudo dmidecode -t 16 | grep -E "Maximum Capacity|Number Of Devices"
!   Maximum Capacity: 32 GB || lo máximo que acepta la placa
!   Number Of Devices: 4 || ranuras en total

$ sudo dmidecode -t memory | grep -E "^\s+(Size|Locator|Type|Speed):"
  Size: 4 GB
  Locator: DIMM1
  Type: DDR3 || compra el mismo tipo
  Speed: 1600 MT/s
?   Size: No Module Installed || ranura libre
  Locator: DIMM2
  Type: Unknown
  Speed: Unknown
  Size: 4 GB
  Locator: DIMM3
  Type: DDR3
  Speed: 1600 MT/s
?   Size: No Module Installed || ranura libre
  Locator: DIMM4
  Type: Unknown
  Speed: Unknown
